These figures are estimates only and are not a mortgage offer. Fixed-rate payments use Canadian semi-annual compounding. Variable-rate payments use monthly compounding. The penalty for a fixed mortgage is shown as the higher of 3 months' interest or a simplified IRD estimate. Lenders calculate penalties differently, so always confirm yours with your current lender.
